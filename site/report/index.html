What the scan checks

Three layers, in the order an agent meets your site. Act checks only count when agents are meant to complete tasks on it, so a marketing site isn't marked down for not having an API.

How the score works

Each check is weighted by how much agents depend on it. A warning earns half. Checks that don't apply to your site, or couldn't be tested, aren't counted.

Questions

Do you need access to my site?

No. The scan only makes the same public requests an agent would: robots.txt, pages, structured data, well-known files and any public API. Nothing behind a login, no forms, no purchases.

Why isn't the Act layer always scored?

Act checks, like an API or an MCP server, only matter if agents are supposed to complete tasks on your site. We detect that, and you can change it on the report. The score updates straight away.

Will a higher score bring more traffic?

We can't promise that. The score measures whether agents can technically find, read and use your site. The research shows a correlation with being recommended, not a guarantee.

Is this certified by AgentReady?

No. The checks follow the open AgentReady specification, but the specification doesn't certify sites or endorse any scanner's score.
