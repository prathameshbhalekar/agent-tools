Deploy the worker

  1. Click Deploy to Cloudflare on the worker's README.
  2. When prompted, set API_KEY to the key above.
  3. Set APP_API_URL to .
  4. In Cloudflare, attach the worker to your API: Workers & Pages → agent-helper → Settings → Domains & Routes → Add route, e.g. api.acme.com/*. Your API's domain must be on Cloudflare.
  5. Add routes on the Routes tab. Clients keep calling your API as before. The worker charges for or logs the matching requests and passes the rest through.

Is your domain not on Cloudflare? After deploying, add an ORIGIN_URL variable pointing at your API (Settings → Variables and Secrets), and have clients call the worker's URL instead.
